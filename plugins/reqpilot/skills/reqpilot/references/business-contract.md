# Shared source and delivery contract

These are the common invariants. Read [the normal route and case index](reading-guide.md); open only relevant case guides and tool-input sections. Case examples are invented, not customer answers or target counts. Explicit current user choices govern scope; historical examples cannot override them.

## Source and objects

The authorized input role determines whether a file is customer source, internal evidence or a previous review. Format and normative language do not change that role. Preserve original bytes/language, source positions and actual ownership. Extraction/OCR, Agent interpretation and engineer decisions remain distinguishable.

Keep ordered, recursive document containment separate from engineering derivation and references. Pure headings and whole covers/contents/document-control blocks remain readable structure without per-field SRC. Other meaningful original items receive SRC, including retained non-requirements. A body obligation on a front page still needs its actual source scope.

Numbered body content defaults to its lowest meaningful original numbered items. Group numbered parts only when they jointly form one complete analyzable item, such as one BOM branch or one test method; preserve internal numbering/composition and explain why. Unnumbered prose follows complete meaning, not punctuation or physical blocks. A 1:1 structure/item shares a visible row; genuine 1:N content keeps its real children.

The requirement subset receives REQ with the corresponding SRC suffix. Preserve customer numbers independently, including gaps and duplicates. Existing identities and retired numbers do not change with sorting, filtering, revisions or exports. Newly discovered earlier content can have a later stable ID and an earlier reading position.

## Judgment and evidence

Classify the governed obligation: FUNCTIONAL, QUALITY or CONSTRAINT under product/system; PROJECT and PROCESS alongside. Content role, topic, applicability and analysis readiness are separate. A numeric value, heading, modal word, table or image does not determine class. Examples and difficult distinctions are in [judgment cases](judgment-cases.md).

Preserve complete original parents, governing conditions, exceptions and precisely scoped shared sources. Derive Snn only within authorized scope when separate responses are useful; never invent obligations or convert original numbered children into engineering derivation. Absence of Snn does not prove a keep-whole review happened.

No supplied internal evidence means skip automatic compliance analysis. An applicable assessment with an item-specific evidence gap may be pending; conflicting or undefined customer criteria need clarification. Neither outcome is compliance. Quotation responses are not measured test results or customer approval.

## Results and revisions

Publish inspectable structure before items/classification and honor only an explicitly requested, version-bound structure confirmation. Otherwise complete the requested scope autonomously: read, compile, check original evidence, repair supported errors and export the current best draft. Feedback is optional intervention, not a default completion gate. Partial source/tool failures identify the affected range; continue all other work and disclose the remaining impact. An inability to encode a correct range is a tool limitation, not permission to change source meaning.

UI and program Excel consume the same version, objects, order, relationships and effective responses. Tables containing images retain their table identity; source pixels, visual readings and derivations remain separate. Normal Excel export needs no new model reading or interpretation.

Preserve previous versions and unaffected decisions. Changed source/context invalidates only dependent judgments; never invent human acceptance or revive stale opinions automatically. Byte/coverage checks prove specified integrity, not semantic correctness or generalization.
